# BetterTot 0.1.0

BetterTot is a native macOS menu-bar scratchpad built for immediate keyboard
capture. It keeps seven independent local pads and saves edits automatically.

## Highlights

- Seven persistent scratchpads with keyboard switching and per-pad undo.
- Configurable global shortcut; the default is Option-Command-Space.
- Pinning, selection and scroll restoration, and multi-display positioning.
- Crash recovery through an append-only journal and atomic pad commits.
- Hourly, daily, and manual local backups.
- Plain-text import, export, and backup restoration.
- Font, spelling, smart quote, smart dash, appearance, and launch-at-login
  settings.
- VoiceOver labels and pad-change announcements.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or later.
- Apple silicon or Intel Mac. The application is distributed as a universal
  `arm64` and `x86_64` binary.

## Installation

Download `BetterTot-0.1.0.zip`, verify it against
`BetterTot-0.1.0.sha256`, extract it, and move `BetterTot.app` to
`/Applications`. Launch it once before enabling launch at login.

## Upgrade Safety

Quit BetterTot before replacing the application in `/Applications`. Notes and
backups remain under `~/Library/Application Support/BetterTot/` and are not
stored inside the application bundle.

## Privacy

BetterTot has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or network sync.
Pads, journals, and backups remain on the Mac in plain-text local storage. See
[the privacy documentation](../PRIVACY.md) for retention details.

## Known Limitations

- Pad names and colors are persisted internally but are not editable in this
  release.
- Backups and recovered journal versions remain until manually removed.
- Sync and automatic crash reporting are not included.
- Command-Left Arrow and Command-Right Arrow switch pads rather than moving the
  text cursor to the beginning or end of a line.
