BetterTot

A scratchpad in your menu bar.

Open it, write something down, close it.

Download for macOS 5 downloads

Version 0.3.1 · macOS 13 or later

01 / Instant capture

The thought is still warm.

BetterTot lives in the menu bar, ready for the sentence, link, or reminder that would otherwise disappear.

02 / Seven pads

Seven places to keep context.

Name and color each pad, then move between them with Command-1 through Command-7. No folder tree to maintain.

03 / Markdown and checklists

Structure without ceremony.

Headings, lists, links, and checkboxes stay readable in the editor and useful when you take the text somewhere else.

04 / Local first

Private by default. Backed up quietly.

Notes stay on your Mac. BetterTot keeps rolling snapshots in iCloud Drive without an account, a sync service, or telemetry.

Ready when you are

A small place to put things.

The current release is ad hoc signed and not notarized. Verify the published SHA-256 checksum, then use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway if macOS blocks it. Never disable Gatekeeper.
